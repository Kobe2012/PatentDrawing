# Example: Photo to Patent-Style Line Drawing

**Goal:** Produce a monochrome draft showing the visible structure of a manual citrus press.

**Input:** A clear photo of a citrus press taken by you or used with permission. Prefer a three-quarter view with the lever, pivot, cone, bowl, and base visible.

## Copyable prompt

```text
Convert the uploaded reference image of a manual citrus press into a clean
black-and-white patent-style perspective line drawing. Preserve the relative
shape and attachment of the long lever, pivot joint, pressing cone, bowl,
upright frame, and base. White background, consistent thin technical outlines,
no texture or shadows. Do not invent unseen fasteners or mechanisms.
```

## What to review

- The lever connects to the correct pivot.
- The cone stays aligned with the bowl.
- The number, placement, and shape of visible components match the source.
- The AI has not silently redesigned the press.
