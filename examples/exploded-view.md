# Example: Exploded-View Illustration

**Goal:** Show how a reusable filter bottle's removable parts fit together.

**Input:** Your own assembly description or reference drawing covering bottle body, screw cap, filter cartridge, and sealing ring.

## Copyable prompt

```text
Create a black-and-white exploded-view technical illustration of a reusable
filter water bottle. Show four components, separated along the vertical
assembly axis: screw cap, sealing ring, removable cylindrical filter cartridge,
and bottle body. Make each component distinct and keep their connection order
consistent with the supplied design. Use fine line art on a white background.
Do not invent internal geometry or assembly features not specified here.
```

## What to review

- Assembly order is correct.
- Components are aligned to an intelligible assembly axis.
- The sealing ring and cartridge are not duplicated or omitted.
- The illustration does not imply unverified internal details.

**Try the product:** https://patentdrawing.app/

**Note:** A visually plausible exploded image is not evidence of assembly fit, dimensional accuracy, or patent-office readiness.
