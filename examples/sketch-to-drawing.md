# Example: Sketch to Patent-Style Drawing

**Goal:** Convert a rough adjustable desk-lamp concept into a readable technical drawing draft.

**Input:** A sketch showing a circular base, two articulated arms, two pivots, and a lampshade.

## Copyable prompt

```text
Use the uploaded hand-drawn adjustable desk lamp as the reference. Create a
monochrome patent-style perspective line drawing with clean continuous
outlines. Preserve the circular base, lower and upper arms, pivot positions,
and shade orientation as sketched. Ignore pencil construction lines, but do
not change the mechanism or invent new joints. Use a white background.
```

## What to review

- The angle and length relationship of both arms match the sketch.
- All pivots are attached, rather than floating.
- Construction marks were removed without erasing actual features.
- The resulting draft is clearly identifiable as the same concept.

**Try the workflow:** https://patentdrawing.app/tools/sketch-to-patent-drawing

**Note:** A rough sketch may not fully specify the mechanism; resolve ambiguity before treating the image as a technical record.
